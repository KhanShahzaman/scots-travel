Rate Logic Summary

This is the short version. It explains how pricing is set up, how a quote is built, and why the final fare changes.

State first Vehicle-specific rates Progressive slabs Extra fees Tax at the end

The pricing story

1
First we create the vehicle.
This holds the base vehicle details such as name, code, seats and luggage.
2
Then we create the state.
This lets the business manage pricing separately by region.
3
Then we add the rate for that vehicle inside that state.
This is where miles, slabs, airport fee, waypoint fee, meet & greet, night fee and tax are defined.
4
When a customer requests a quote, the pickup location decides the state.
The system then loads all active vehicle rates for that state.
5
Each vehicle gets its own fare.
The customer sees the options and picks one.
Important: the same vehicle can be reused across multiple states, but each state can have its own completely different price for that vehicle.

How the fare is built

The fare starts with mileage, but mileage is not charged as one flat rate for the full trip. It uses slabs.

Example: the first 10 miles can be charged at one rate, and any miles after that can be charged at another.

Simple way to read it: one journey can move through more than one mileage band.

After mileage, the system can add airport pickup fee, waypoint fee, meet & greet fee, night surcharge, and finally tax.

This is the admin screen where one vehicle's price is configured inside a selected state.
